What the toy simplifies

  • The trust-region rule is AFC's. As in Scvx6DofSolver.Iterate: a step is kept when ρ > 0; η halves when ρ < 0.25, rejected or not; η grows ×1.5 when ρ ≥ 0.7 and the step used at least 80 % of η; otherwise it stays. η is clamped between a floor and a cap a hundred times larger (AFC: 10⁻³ and 0.1; here 0.01 and 1), and a cold solve starts at the cap.
  • AFC's trust region is a box, not a ball. Every state, control and the burn and coast times must stay within η of the reference, each on its own scale: |x − x̄| ≤ η·scale per variable, an ∞-norm ball. "Used" is the largest such scaled change. The toy uses a 2-norm ball so that its slice on the plane is a circle.
  • AFC prices the defect with a quadratic penalty. Its merit (TrueCost) is the fuel used + small quadratic prices on control changes, body rates, RCS and fins + ρvc Σ (defect / scale)² over every interval and state channel, with ρvc = 10⁵ and the defect taken from the true nonlinear dynamics, + the L1 prices of the soft acceleration and turn-rate limits. The predicted merit prices the subproblem's virtual control the same way. The toy uses an exact ℓ1 penalty, λ‖g‖₁, which puts the creases in the landscape.
  • AFC's subproblems can fail. SCS can stop at its iteration cap. The solver can retry the same subproblem once with a larger cap; the flight guidance sets its caps so that retry does not fire. A solve that is still truncated, unusable, or outside its time bounds is a failed iteration: no step, and η halves. The toy's subproblem always solves.
  • Convergence. AFC stops on a kept step whose largest scaled change is under 8·10⁻³ while the largest scaled defect is under 10⁻³. The toy stops when the model predicts a drop under 10⁻⁴.

The problem: minimise ½Σ wi(xi − ci)² + λ‖g(x)‖₁ over x ∈ ℝ⁸, with three nonlinear defects gj(x) = aj·x + 1.4 sin(bj·x) + 0.5 (dj·x)² − ej and λ = 6. Each iteration linearises g at xk, minimises the convex model inside a 2-norm ball of radius η, and keeps or refuses the step by the ratio ρ of actual to predicted improvement, with AFC's rule above. Axes are distances in x along the two orthonormal plane directions. Hollow markers are iterates that lie off the plane, drawn at their projection.
