Solver Cell

The SCvx solver shown as a living cell. Its size is the trust region, and each heartbeat is one iteration: it reaches out with a step, then absorbs the reach if the step helped or snaps it back if it did not. The runs are recorded from AFC's own solvers, including two landings flown in real time at the game's 10 Hz guidance rate.

Recordedfrom v0.16.0, 2026-11-16, before the plan had RCS, fins and the coast phase

How to read it

  • Size is the trust region: how far the solver trusts its simplified model. It halves after a poor prediction (ρ under 0.25), grows ×1.5 after a good one (ρ at least 0.7) that used most of the radius, and never passes its cap.
  • Reaching is the step it tries. The reach is absorbed when the step is kept and snaps back when it is rejected. It points at the constraint it was about: what a kept step fixed most, or what went wrong in a refused one.
  • Wobble is surprise: how far the real result landed from what the model predicted.
  • Bulges show which constraints the trajectory still breaks, such as velocity or attitude for a landing.
  • Pressed flat against a wall: the step used the whole trust region and the model was right, so the solver widens it. The solver wants to move further than it allows.
  • A red flash and a glitch mean the solver refused a step: the real merit did not fall at all (ρ ≤ 0), whatever the model promised. A step that falls short of its promise but still helps is kept.
  • Colour is how badly the trajectory breaks physics against the solver's tolerance: calm under a tenth of it, amber at it, red at ten times it.
  • Grey and still means the solver gave up.

Recorded headless on 2026-11-16 from AFC main (v0.16.0), before the 6-DOF plan had RCS, fins and the coast phase; the trust-region rules it ran are the ones on main now. The two flights fly the 6-DOF regression landing in real time: one cold solve, then every 0.1 s the plan is shifted forward, re-seeded warm with a 0.05 trust region as the game does, and re-solved with up to 3 (rough ride: 2) iterations, with tracking noise and gusts added to the state. They play at the real guidance rate at 1×; the cold solve plays at the time it took here, which is not representative of a gaming PC, and the warm loop ran without the game's 25 ms deadline. The other runs are the regression landing, the same landing with three times the sideways offset and with a burn-time guess four times too long, and the convex ascent on its Saturn V case, at one iteration per beat. Size maps the trust radius on a log scale between the solver's limits. A bulge maps that constraint family's worst defect on a log scale from 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻¹, half grown at the 10⁻³ convergence tolerance. The colour uses the worst family against the same tolerance: calm up to 10⁻⁴, amber at 10⁻³, red at 10⁻². The wall appears on every step that grew the trust region, which both solvers allow only when the model predicted well and the step used at least 80 % of the radius. The cell reaches toward a constraint family, at the angle of its bulge: for a kept step, the family whose worst defect fell the most (at least a tenth of a decade), otherwise the one that rose the most; for a refused step, the solver's own worst channel, or the family that rose the most. A step that changes nothing much keeps the last direction. Steps limited by the controls, such as most wall presses near touchdown, have no family of their own yet, because the change in the controls is not logged. How far it lunges is the step size.
