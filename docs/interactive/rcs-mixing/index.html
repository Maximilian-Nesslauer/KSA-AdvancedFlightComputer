RCS mixing

How AFC turns a torque demand into the cheapest set of thruster duties its layout allows, what side force comes with it, and how the vehicle worker turns those duties into pulses.

The programme

minΣi ṁi di + Σa p wa (e+a + e−a) s.t.Σi ti di + e+ − e− = τ 0 ≤ di ≤ 1 (through a slack di + si = 1),  e± ≥ 0
di
Thruster i's duty: the share of the 0.1 s control period it fires. These are what the mixer solves for, one per thruster.
ṁi
Its propellant flow at full thrust, kg/s. This is the price of a duty, so the mixer picks the pattern that burns the least propellant.
ti
The torque it gives at full thrust about the centre of mass, ri × fi, N m, in pitch, yaw and roll.
τ
The torque the caller asks for. On this page it is the red ring you drag.
e±
The shortfall: the part of the demand no duty pattern can give. It is split into a positive and a negative part per axis so both stay non-negative.
p
The shortfall price, 103 × maxi ṁi/|ti|: a thousand times the dearest thruster's propellant per newton metre. The mixer gives every newton metre it can before it leaves any unmet.
wa
The axis priorities, (1, 1, 0.3) from every caller: pitch and yaw before roll. When the demand is out of reach, the axis with the smallest weight gives way first.
fi
Its full-thrust force. The side force Σi fi di is not a constraint. It is left free and reported, because thrusters at the top of a booster cannot turn it without pushing it sideways.
Then the pulses. A thruster is on or off. At the start of each period of T = 0.1 s, thruster i wants diT + ci seconds of firing, ci being what it carried from the last period: want = dᵢ·T + cᵢ if want ≥ minimum pulse: fire min(want, T), carry min(want − fired, T) else: fire nothing, carry want So a small duty comes out as an occasional pulse with the right average, not a minimum pulse every period and not nothing. Duties under 10−4 are dropped before this.

Pulses

One row per thruster, one cell per 0.1 s period, starting from no carry as a fresh command would. Each cell shows the pulse fired and the time carried into the next period. Slide the minimum pulse up and the small duties stop firing every period: they save up and fire a minimum pulse now and then, with the same average.
